## Best Practices

1. **Hook in 3 seconds**: Start with something attention-grabbing
2. **Trending audio**: Use popular sounds to boost reach
3. **Text overlays**: Make content accessible without sound
4. **Call to action**: Always direct to link in bio
5. **Consistent branding**: Use same colors/style across reels
6. **Engage with comments**: Reply to build community
7. **Post consistently**: 3-5 reels per week
8. **Test and iterate**: See what works and do more of it
9. **Use stories**: Drive traffic from stories to reels
10. **Collaborate**: Partner with creators in tech/productivity space
